Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How are the fees calculated?

Our fee primarily covers the buddy's service time. Any additional costs incurred during the experience, such as food, transportation, or entrance tickets for both you and your buddy, are your responsibility unless specified otherwise. We believe in transparent pricing.

What's the difference between a buddy and a tour guide?

This is a key point. Our "buddies" are not licensed tour guides and do not provide standardized historical explanations of attractions. They act as your local friends, offering personalized companionship, cultural exchange, and a taste of everyday life. This model provides a deeper cultural immersion but is not an official tour guide service.

How is safety ensured?

We conduct identity verification and interviews for all student applicants. We also recommend that all meetings take place in public areas and provide basic accident insurance for your experience.

What if I need to cancel?

We understand that plans can change. You can cancel for free up to 48 hours before your scheduled experience. Please refer to our terms of service for more details.
