Our Story

Shanghai Local Buddy was founded on a simple idea: travel should be about human connection, not just visiting attractions. Our founder, Jacob Zhang, experienced this firsthand during a trip abroad where a local student friend showed him an authentic side of the culture he would have never seen otherwise. He realized this "friend-led" model was the most beautiful way to travel.

Upon returning to Shanghai, he decided to bring this idea to life. He connected talented and enthusiastic university students with foreign tourists eager to genuinely understand the city, creating the Shanghai Local Buddy platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to break down the barriers between tourists and locals, fostering cross-cultural understanding through a platform built on sincerity and friendship. We are dedicated to providing the most authentic Shanghai experiences, turning every trip into an unforgettable story of friendship.

What Makes Us Different?

  • No Middlemen: We are a connection platform, not a traditional travel agency. You communicate directly with your buddy.
  • No Tourist Traps: Our buddies will never take you to commission-based shopping stops. All plans are based on your interests.
  • Deeply Personalized: Every experience can be tailored to your pace and preferences.
  • Support Local Students: Your payment is not just for a service; it's an appreciation and support for the value that local university students provide.
